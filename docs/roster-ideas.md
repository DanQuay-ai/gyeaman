# Next heroes: four archetypes the roster is missing

Current eight: sword (Blay), night blade/assassin (Dufie), spear lancer (Kuukua), archer (Obiri), shield tank (Gyakari), axe berserker (Afrakoma), fire priestess (Kome), twin-axe bruiser (Owureku).
Missing roles: life-drain, unarmed martial artist, team support/tempo, poison/attrition.

## 1. Bayibɔnsam (male) — energy vampire / drainer
- Role: sustain duelist. Every hit steals health or energy; strongest in long fights.
- Look: lean, hollow-cheeked man, dark indigo and ash-grey robes with frayed hems, bone and cowrie charms, pale ash markings, long nails; no weapon, or a short hooked iron claw with an afena grip.
- Kit ideas: Siphon (melee touch that heals for the damage), Drain Mist (a dark cloud that drains each turn), Hollow Pact (steals the enemy's energy/turn meter), Ult: Night Feast (tendrils pull the enemy in, drains a large chunk and heals).
- Effects: violet-black tendrils and particles flowing FROM the enemy TO him; afterimages in deep violet.

## 2. Anadwo Sekan (male) — hand-to-hand kickboxer
- Role: fast combo striker, many small hits, builds energy quickly.
- Look: wiry, shaved sides, black wraps on hands and shins, night-blue fighting trousers, bare chest with a gold neck ring echoing Dufie's order.
- Kit ideas: Jab-Cross-Hook chain, Spinning Back Kick (knock-back), Knee Rush (dash knee, stun), Ult: Midnight Flurry (dozens of punches and kicks ending in an axe kick).
- Effects: short crisp impact stars per hit, speed lines, violet afterimages like Dufie.

## 3. Ɔkyerɛma (talking drummer) — support / tempo
- Role: buffs and turn-order control; sonic projectiles.
- Look: broad-shouldered drummer with an atumpan talking drum slung at his side, kente sash, beaded cap.
- Kit ideas: War Rhythm (speed up), Summons Beat (cleanse + small heal), Thunder Roll (sonic rings that push the enemy back on the turn meter), Ult: Ancestral Call (a chorus of spectral drummers; big damage and team-wide speed).
- Effects: expanding golden sound rings, Adinkra symbols pulsing on the beat.

## 4. Sumankwaafoɔ (herbalist) — poison and attrition
- Role: damage over time, debuffs, healing reduction.
- Look: slender herbalist with gourds, leaf pouches and a carved staff with an afena head, green and earth tones.
- Kit ideas: Thorn Dart (poison), Bitter Root (heal reduction + slow), Leaf Veil (evade), Ult: Forest Rot (poison cloud that ticks hard; bonus on already-poisoned targets).
- Effects: green spore clouds, thorn projectiles, creeping vines.
